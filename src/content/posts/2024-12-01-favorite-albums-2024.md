Every year I try to keep track of music that resonates. Here are a few albums from 2024 that I kept coming back to.

## The List

1. **Waxahatchee - Tigers Blood**
   Indie rock with a warm, lived-in feel. Perfect for long drives.

2. **Mkwaju Ensemble - Mkwaju**
   Reissue of 1980s Kenyan electronic music. Hypnotic and strange in the best way.

3. **Adrianne Lenker - Bright Future**
   Sparse, intimate folk. Her voice and guitar, mostly unadorned.

4. **SAULT - EARTH**
   Afrofuturist soul that feels both ancient and futuristic.

5. **The Smile - Wall of Eyes**
   Radiohead adjacent, but looser and more experimental.

## Themes

Looking at this list, I realize I gravitated toward music that felt handmade and human this year. Less production gloss, more raw texture.

Maybe that's a reaction to how much time I spent staring at screens. Music became a refuge from the algorithmic feed.
